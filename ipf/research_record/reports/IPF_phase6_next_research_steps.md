# IPF Open Evidence Record — Phase 6: Next Research Steps

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Scope:** Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis only · **Status:** final phase of the workflow

## What Phase 6 was

Phase 4 ended with **zero surviving research leads**. Phase 5 (deep investigation of surviving
leads) was therefore recorded as *skipped — nothing to investigate*, not as *not done*.
Phase 6 executed the nine actions that Phase 4 had carried forward, under a standing rule:
execute rather than recommend wherever accessible evidence permits, and where it does not,
state exactly what is missing and name the smallest concrete step that would obtain it.

No new candidate hunting was performed. The Phase 4 screens were applied unchanged.

### What was closed

- **A5 (registry-results sweep)** — the largest single correction to the record. The posted-results
  sections of all 116 IPF trials with results on ClinicalTrials.gov were read. This produced 11
  corrections (C24–C34), four reclassifications to *Failed*, several to *Uncertain*, three duplicate
  merges, and a new evidence class: **results that exist but cannot be graded** — posted to a registry
  with no between-arm analysis, no publication, or both.
- **A3 (missing-results retrieval)** — of 110 completed trials with no retrievable result, a targeted
  registry-identifier search found any indexed publication for only 7, and **none** reported the missing
  primary clinical outcome. The non-reporting deficit is real, not a search artefact. Two entities were
  added to the landscape (SRN001, an amphiregulin siRNA; an AI-discovered TNIK inhibitor), both phase 1
  safety and pharmacokinetics only.
- **A7 (corticosteroid harm magnitude)** — answered by an existing synthesis rather than re-pooled.
  Ten observational studies in 1639 patients give an uncertain mortality effect (RR 1.03, 95% CI 0.85–1.25)
  and, in IPF, an uncertain FVC effect (−3.80% predicted, 95% CI −8.94 to 1.34), both very low certainty.
  Acute-exacerbation cohorts disagree interpretably: no in-hospital mortality association but worse overall
  survival in one propensity-weighted cohort, no advantage of pulse over non-pulse dosing, and better
  survival with early tapering. **Confounding by indication is not resolved by any of them.**
- **A9 (measurement development)** — five candidate endpoints were specified for the dimensions this record
  cannot currently measure: reversal of established fibrosis, recovery of lost lung function, gas-transfer
  recovery, durable benefit after an exacerbation, and anchored functional recovery. Every one of the five
  smallest validation steps is a re-analysis of existing images or datasets, not a new trial. **None is validated.**

### What hit the limit of accessible evidence

- **A1 (PRECISIONS)** — completed 2026-03-02, no posted results, only a design paper indexed. This is the
  genotype-stratified trial that the *TOLLIP* × N-acetylcysteine interaction paper explicitly asked for, and it
  decides the fate of candidate P10. Next step: recheck after the reporting deadline (2027-03-02).
  Until then, nothing about N-acetylcysteine in rs3750920 TT patients may be restated.
- **A2 (closed-access retrieval)** — the *TOLLIP* paper's open deposit holds abstract only; its numbers are now
  verified against the primary abstract, but subgroup sizes and prespecification remain unverifiable. The
  Kangxian Huanji Granule trial has no open copy, though its full structured abstract established the design
  for the first time (randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, n=80; treatment failure RR 0.22, 95% CI
  0.051–0.965; **all-cause mortality RR 0.75, 95% CI 0.179–3.138 — null**).
- **A4 (Chinese-language coverage)** — ChiCTR and the WHO ICTRP portal were both granted at the network level;
  ICTRP refuses non-browser clients and ChiCTR's search requires an encrypted, bot-protected session. Neither was
  circumvented. Instead the gap was **measured** from English-indexed reviews that had searched CNKI, Wanfang, VIP
  and CBM: at least 63 randomised trials of Chinese medicine injections, 23 trials in 1390 patients of
  qi-supplementing herbs, 20 trials in 1363 patients across four injection products, 11 trials in 981 patients of
  external TCM treatment, and a herbal-versus-N-acetylcysteine comparison in 733 patients. **No injection product
  appears anywhere in the 279-intervention landscape.** No verdict is assigned to any of this evidence.
- **A6 (co-trimoxazole adherence)** — not executable from published summaries; requires individual participant
  dosing records. Priority is low: two independent randomised trials are null, so the analysis would test whether
  a null was caused by non-adherence, not test a positive signal.
- **A8 (FVC-to-survival surrogacy)** — the published literature answers a different question. Patient-level
  prognostic association is established (HR 1.79, 95% CI 1.57–2.03 per 5-point faster decline, n=2583; registry
  hazards of 1.8-, 2.3- and 2.7-fold after declines of ≥2%, ≥5%, ≥10% predicted). **Treatment-effect surrogacy —
  whether a drug's effect on FVC predicts its effect on survival — is not established by any published analysis.**
  Every "slows progression" verdict in this record rests on FVC, so this gap propagates to all of them.

## Effect on the record

- Landscape: **283 rows, 4 marked duplicates, 279 distinct interventions** — 7 *Works*, 51 *Uncertain*,
  17 *Failed*, 9 *Harm outweighs benefit*, 195 *Poorly studied*.
- Corrections log: **38 entries**, of which 15 were written in Phase 6 (C24–C38).
- Treatment map: **89 statements**; Phase 6 added 11, including the two new record-completeness findings
  and the two analysis findings above.
- Candidate register: 8 new candidates screened, **0 promoted**. The register's promoted-to-lead column
  remains uniformly negative.
- New limitations L6 (registry-only evidence class; single-registry sweep) and L7 (partial
  traditional-medicine sample).

## The honest bottom line

**Zero research leads survive.** Phase 6 did not change that, and it was not designed to.
What it changed is the record's accuracy: four agents previously graded as untested are now graded
as failed, a whole class of registry-only evidence is now named rather than silently mixed with
published evidence, the non-reporting problem is quantified rather than asserted, and the single
largest block of unexamined randomised evidence — Chinese-language trials of injection products —
is located and measured without being credited.

Two positive-direction findings remain **held at verification** rather than rejected: an open-label
combination regimen for acute exacerbations and a single-site nasal-spray cough result. Both are held
for the same reason — their only sources lack the analyses needed to interpret them — and both have a
named retrieval action rather than a proposed trial.

The most consequential single fact in this phase is that the trial which would resolve the record's
best-documented subgroup signal is **complete and unreported**. No amount of further searching
substitutes for that result being released.
