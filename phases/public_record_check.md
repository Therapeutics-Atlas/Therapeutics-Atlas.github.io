# Public-Record Check Before Contacting Researchers

Scope: verification of what the two Phase 6 next steps can be executed from public sources alone.
No researchers were contacted. No new literature review, no new lead search, no trial design.
Full per-source table: ra_public_record_check.csv

---

## RL-01 — TREAT EARLIER individual-participant data

**Can the step be executed from public information? No. Direct contact is required.**

**Data-sharing statement.** Two publications carry effectively the same statement. The 4-year
paper (Lancet Rheumatology 2024): requests for TREAT EARLIER data, such as deidentified
participant data, can be made to the corresponding author of that Article on publication, and
requests will be considered on an individual basis. The MRI responder analysis — the paper whose
design created this lead's uncertainty — states the same: available on reasonable request to its
corresponding author following publication, considered individually. Neither names a repository,
a data access committee, or an application form.

**The protocol is more restrictive than the journals.** Item {29} of the published protocol
(Trials 2020) commits the final trial dataset to the principal investigator and data manager at
the LUMC Department of Rheumatology. There is no protocol-level provision for third-party access.
The journals' case-by-case offer is therefore the only route, and it is discretionary rather than
contractual.

**Contact route.** Fully determined from the public record: each of the two papers names a
corresponding author at the LUMC Department of Rheumatology, with the institutional email printed
in the article; the senior/PI author (van der Helm-van Mil, LUMC and Erasmus MC) is on both. The
MRI responder analysis has the more recent contact and is the paper the proposed re-analysis
directly extends.

**Eligibility and conditions.** No published criteria exist. What the public record does show is
the group's own methodological precedent — protocol and statistical analysis plan for the 2-year
analyses published in advance — which indicates a preregistered analysis plan should accompany any
request. The EU register (EudraCT 2014-004472-35) records end-of-trial status Completed with a
global end date of 2024-10-10, i.e. the trial closed recently and the dataset may still be under
active analysis by the original team; that is a realistic obstacle to a third-party request, not a
formal one.

**Are the required variables available?** The variables the interaction test needs are collected and
publicly defined. The protocol documents MRI scoring for synovitis and osteitis by OMERACT RAMRIS
extended to the MTP joints, tenosynovitis by the Haavardsholm method, with a detailed scoring
overview in supplementary material; it defines the primary endpoint (clinically detectable
arthritis / 2010 criteria), the co-primary DMARD-free status endpoint, the patient-reported
secondary endpoints, and blinded allocation. What is **not** public is the granularity of any
dataset that would actually be shared — whether per-joint RAMRIS component scores would be
released or only the composite burden variable and the published binary responder definition. A
treatment-by-continuous-burden interaction needs the former; the placebo-arm application of the
published responder definition needs only the latter.

**Conclusion.** Every access condition is now documented from public sources, and no repository
route exists. The step is executable only via a discretionary request to a named corresponding
author, and the request should specify the variable granularity required and carry a preregistered
analysis plan.

## RL-02 — Status of the TRAFIC phase 2a efficacy stage

**Can the step be executed from public information? No. The question remains unresolved after
checking five classes of public record; direct contact is required.**

Five source classes were checked; none reports a phase 2a enrolment or result.

1. **Trial registry (ISRCTN36667085, full record).** Target enrolment 39 (part 1 up to 21 plus part 2 n=18); **total final enrolment 15**; recruitment 2014-10-01 to **2022-06-20**; overall end date 2023-12-31; publication stage "Results"; IPD sharing plan "No". Linked outputs are the phase 1b results article (n=15), the protocol, the statistical analysis plan, and an HRA summary — nothing from part 2.
2. **EU register.** The HRA record supplies EudraCT 2014-001339-35, which was not previously in hand. The EU Clinical Trials Register returns no record for it: adult-only phase 1 trials outside an agreed paediatric investigation plan are not publicly displayed. Dead end.
3. **Second registry.** No ClinicalTrials.gov record for seliciclib or roscovitine in RA. ISRCTN is the sole registry.
4. **Funder record (UKRI Gateway to Research, MRC MR/L005123/1).** Title: "MICA: Targeting the RA synovial fibroblast via cyclin dependent kinase inhibition — a phase IIa study"; status Closed. The reported trial outcome cites only the phase 1b, analysis-plan and protocol papers and links back to the ISRCTN record. The grant that funded the phase IIa closed with no phase IIa output reported to the funder.
5. **Institutional/regulatory record (HRA research summary, IRAS 151861).** Sponsor: The Newcastle upon Tyne Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust; REC North East – Tyne & Wear South, reference 14/NE/1075, favourable opinion 15 Sep 2014; named contact J Isaacs (Newcastle). The summary describes part 2 as planned — the recommended dose given to 18 patients for 12 weeks with imaging and synovial biopsies — and contains no end-of-study or results section.

**What the public record now establishes.** The absence of a phase 2a result is not a retrieval
gap: no publication, preprint or indexed abstract exists, the funder received no such output, and
the grant is closed. The registry's final enrolment of 15 equals the phase 1b cohort exactly.

**What it still cannot establish.** Whether part 2 ever opened to recruitment, whether any
participant received the recommended dose for 12 weeks, and why the stage did not report. One
fact keeps this genuinely open rather than settled: registered recruitment ran to June 2022,
roughly fourteen months after the phase 1b paper published and described the efficacy evaluation
as ongoing, yet no output from that period exists and the enrolment field was never incremented.
That is consistent either with part 2 opening and failing to recruit, or with the registry field
never being updated — the public record does not discriminate between them, and a registry field
is not evidence of what happened in clinic.

**Enquiry route.** Named by the phase 1b paper's own data-sharing statement: data beyond the
manuscript and appendix are withheld for commercial sensitivity, and should that change requests
will be considered by the research team including Newcastle Clinical Trials Unit and the Chief
Investigator. The HRA record independently confirms sponsor, ethics reference and institutional
contact.

## Bottom line

Both Phase 6 steps require direct contact with the original research teams. Neither can be
executed from public information.

The public-record work was not wasted in either case. For RL-01 it converts an open-ended request
into a specific one: a named contact, a documented case-by-case condition, a known absence of any
repository or formal access process, confirmation that the needed variables exist and are defined,
and one explicit question to ask (per-joint RAMRIS components, or composites only). For RL-02 it
substantially narrows what the enquiry must ask: the registry, EU register, second registry,
funder outcomes and institutional summary have all been checked and are documented as silent, so
the enquiry reduces to three factual questions — did part 2 open to recruitment; were any
participants dosed at the recommended dose for 12 weeks; and what stopped it.

This is a negative result for both retrieval attempts and is recorded as such. It is not evidence
about whether seliciclib does or does not work in rheumatoid arthritis, and no treatment is
recommended or claimed effective here.
