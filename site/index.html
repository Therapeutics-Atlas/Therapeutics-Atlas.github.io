    About the project

    Each record is built to be examined, not taken on trust. Every load-bearing claim is traced to a primary source where possible; negative and null results are kept rather than discarded; and contradictions are shown rather than resolved away. AI is used as a research assistant, not as a doctor.

    Three layers

    1. 1

      Treatment Map

      What works, what does not, what is uncertain, and what is unknown.

    2. 2

      Research Leads

      Specific unanswered questions or signals that survive critical review.

    3. 3

      Validation

      Only where a lead becomes strong enough to justify further scientific or clinical testing.

    Working principles

    1. Evidence first; where it is insufficient, the record says "we don't know."
    2. No treatment is assumed to work from mechanism alone, nor dismissed simply because its mechanism is not fully understood.
    3. Modern therapies, repurposed drugs, historical treatments, natural products, and traditional medicine are evaluated by the evidence, not by where they come from.
    4. Negative results are results, and are collected deliberately.
    Therapeutics Atlas — an open, correctable research record. Nothing here has been peer reviewed. The full research trail and structured data live alongside each disease record.
